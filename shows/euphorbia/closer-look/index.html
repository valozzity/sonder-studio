Euphorbia: A Closer Look

Theo Hirschfield

Euphorbia

On view

September 3 – November 1, 2026

Gallery Sonder

3435 E. Coast Hwy
Corona Del Mar CA 92625

A focused selection from Euphorbia offers a closer exploration of Hirschfield’s distinctive ceramic forms, where rounded, capsule-like silhouettes conceal unexpected interiors. Richly textured surfaces give way to vibrant color within, creating a playful element of discovery.

Euphorbia remains on view through November 1. We invite you to visit the gallery and experience the exhibition in person.
